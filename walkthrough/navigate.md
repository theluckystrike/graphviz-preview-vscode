# Pan, zoom, find and jump

- Drag the graph to pan. The mouse wheel or trackpad also pans.
- Cmd or Ctrl plus the wheel zooms at the pointer. Fit and 1:1 reset the view. Keys: `+`, `-`, `0`, `1`.
- Type in Find node. Matching nodes light up; Enter goes to the next one.
- Click a node, edge or cluster to put the cursor on its line in the source.
